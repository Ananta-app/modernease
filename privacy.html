Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Official Policy

Your Privacy Matters: ModernEase respects your privacy. We do not sell your personal data, we do not require user accounts to browse our editorial content, and we do not store sensitive payment information.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how ModernEase Living ("ModernEase", "we", "us", or "our"), operating at https://modernease.pages.dev, collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit our website, read our buying guides, or interact with our content.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. We do not require visitors to register accounts, provide passwords, or enter personal billing information.

  • Technical & Log Data: Like most web platforms, our hosting provider (Cloudflare Pages) automatically records standard server log information, including your IP address (anonymized where applicable), browser type, referring/exit pages, operating system, and date/time stamps for security and traffic distribution.
  • Aggregated Analytics: We may analyze aggregated, non-personally identifiable traffic metrics to understand which home and kitchen guides readers find most helpful.
  • Direct Communications: If you choose to contact us via email or our contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message content you provide to respond to your inquiry.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

ModernEase utilizes cookies and similar web technologies for essential operational purposes:

  • Cloudflare Security Cookies: Essential cookies deployed to protect the website against malicious traffic, bot attacks, and DDoS activity.
  • Amazon Associates Affiliate Cookies: When you click on an Amazon link on ModernEase, Amazon places a cookie on your browser. This cookie tracks qualifying purchases for attribution purposes under the Amazon Associates Operating Agreement. These cookies typically have a standard duration of 24 hours (or up to 90 days if an item is added to the shopping cart). You can disable or delete cookies anytime in your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Links & Retailers

Our website contains links to third-party retail destinations, primarily Amazon.com and Pinterest. Once you leave our domain, our privacy policy no longer applies. We strongly encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

5. No Sale of Personal Information (CCPA/CPRA Compliance)

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties. We do not share your personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

ModernEase Living is an editorial lifestyle website directed to adults and homeowners aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13.

7. Data Retention & Security

We implement rigorous modern security standards provided by Cloudflare's global edge network, including automated TLS/SSL encryption. Communications received via email are retained only as long as necessary to address your feedback or inquiry.

8. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data rights, please contact our data team at privacy@modernease.pages.dev or visit our Contact Page.
