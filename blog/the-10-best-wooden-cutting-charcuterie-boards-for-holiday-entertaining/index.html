BUYER'S GUIDE

What to Consider Before You Choose

Material Matters

Choose between end-grain (durable, knife-friendly) and edge-grain (thinner, easier to store). Bamboo is sustainable and lightweight, while maple and teak offer superior density for heavy-duty carving.

Maintenance Routine

Wooden boards are not 'set it and forget it.' To keep them from warping or cracking, hand wash only and apply food-safe mineral oil or board cream every month.

Counter Clearance

Measure the height of your upper cabinets. A massive butcher block is great, but if it doesn't fit under your cabinets for storage or prep, you will eventually find it annoying to use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I put these boards in the dishwasher?

Absolutely not. Dishwasher heat and moisture will cause wood to warp, crack, and lose its finish instantly. Always hand wash and dry immediately.

How do I know if my board needs oiling?

If the wood looks dull, feels 'thirsty' or rough to the touch, or shows signs of graying, it is time for a fresh coat of food-grade mineral oil.

Which wood is best for preventing knife dulling?

End-grain construction is the best for your knives because the wood fibers are oriented vertically, allowing the blade to slip between fibers rather than hitting them head-on.
