BUYER'S GUIDE

What to Consider Before You Choose

Clearance and Height

Before ordering, measure your available space—especially if placing the lamp under low-hanging cabinets or shelves. Aim for a height that doesn't obstruct your view.

Material & Maintenance

Natural materials like marble and glass offer a better tactile feel and longevity, while plastic models are lighter and often more water-resistant.

Battery vs. Plug-in

Always check the mAh (milliampere-hour) rating if you want longer runtime. Higher ratings usually mean you won't have to recharge your ‘daily driver’ lamp as often.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these lamps bright enough to be the primary light in a room?

No, these are ambient accent lamps. They are designed to create a warm, cozy mood, not to replace overhead lighting or high-lumen desk task lamps.

How do I clean the lamps?

For glass and marble, use a damp microfiber cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals on the touch-sensitive base components to ensure longevity.

Does ‘cordless’ mean I never have to plug them in?

No, ‘cordless’ means you can move them freely while in use, but you will need to recharge them periodically via the included USB-C cable.
