BUYER'S GUIDE

What to Consider Before You Choose

Check Your Clearance

Before buying, measure the height between your fridge shelves. Opt for stackable, shallow designs if you have limited vertical space.

Care vs. Convenience

Glass bases are almost always dishwasher-safe, but remember that wooden lids usually require hand-washing to prevent warping. Factor this into your daily cleaning routine.

Seal Integrity

Check if the lids have silicone seals. While bamboo looks great, an airtight silicone ring is necessary for keeping food fresh and preventing fridge odors from seeping in.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are glass containers actually microwave safe?

Yes, high-borosilicate glass is designed for thermal shock resistance. Always remove the bamboo lid before microwaving to avoid damaging the wood.

How do I stop bamboo lids from looking dry over time?

Occasionally wipe them with a food-safe mineral oil or bamboo conditioner to keep the grain hydrated and rich.

Is the silicone seal dishwasher safe?

Most silicone seals are, but check the manufacturer notes. I recommend removing them occasionally to clean any trapped debris for a truly hygienic seal.
