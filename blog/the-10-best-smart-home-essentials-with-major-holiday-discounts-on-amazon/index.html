BUYER'S GUIDE

What to Consider Before You Choose

Check Your Frequency

Most of these devices rely on 2.4GHz Wi-Fi. Ensure your router can support a stable 2.4GHz band before committing to a full home automation overhaul.

Counter Clearance

Before buying a hub or smart display, measure your counter depth. Minimalist design is about having extra space, not filling it up.

Protocol Synergy

If you buy multiple devices, look for those supporting 'Matter.' It is the current gold standard for ensuring your devices actually talk to each other without needing three different apps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a hub for all these products?

Not necessarily. Many items on this list, like the Linkind bulbs, work directly over Wi-Fi, though a hub like Homey Pro is recommended if you plan to scale up to more than 5–10 devices.

Are these devices difficult to install?

Most are 'peel and stick' or plug-and-play. The most complex install is the smart thermostat, which takes about 20 minutes and a screwdriver.

How do I maintain a minimalist look with all this tech?

Hide wires with fabric cable sleeves, mount cameras high on bookshelves, and choose white or matte-black finishes that blend into your existing walls.
