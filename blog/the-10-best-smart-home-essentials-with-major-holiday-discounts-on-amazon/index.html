BUYER'S GUIDE

What to Consider Before You Choose

Matter Compatibility

Always check for the 'Matter' badge. This ensures your devices will communicate across brands, saving you from downloading a dozen different apps.

Outlet Clearance

If you live in a small space, look for 'Ultra-Mini' profiles to ensure your plug doesn't render your second wall outlet useless.

Wi-Fi Frequency

Most smart devices still require a 2.4GHz network for setup. Ensure your router can handle both 2.4GHz and 5GHz to avoid pairing headaches.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I really need a Matter hub?

Yes, if you want your devices to work reliably locally without constant cloud reliance, a Matter-certified hub (like a HomePod, Echo, or dedicated bridge) is recommended.

How do I fix pairing issues?

Always ensure your phone is connected to a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network during initial setup. Once configured, your devices will hold the connection.

Are these plugs safe for high-draw appliances?

Look for 15A rating (like our picks from Kasa and GHome) if you are plugging in heaters or coffee makers. Avoid cheap plugs rated for only 10A.
