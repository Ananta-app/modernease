BUYER'S GUIDE

What to Consider Before You Choose

Vertical Clearance

Measure your distance from counter to upper cabinets; look for blenders under 15 inches if space is tight.

Ease of Cleaning

Prioritize models with auto-clean presets or those with dishwasher-safe components; hidden blades are always a nightmare.

Material Integrity

Glass or BPA-free Tritan is preferred for longevity and preventing residual food odors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can these small blenders actually crush ice?

Yes, but look for motors with at least 800+ peak watts for seamless results without burning out the motor.

Are the blender cups truly leakproof?

In our testing, the Ninja BlendBOSS series offers the most reliable seals for commuting, but always ensure the rubber gasket is seated properly before tightening.

Should I choose glass or plastic?

Glass is heavier and odor-resistant, ideal for a permanent spot on your counter, while Tritan plastic is better for lightweight portability and drop resistance.
