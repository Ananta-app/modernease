BUYER'S GUIDE

What to Consider Before You Choose

Clearance and Sightlines

Ensure the combined height of your holder and candle doesn't obstruct conversation across the table. A good rule of thumb is to keep centerpieces either very low (under 6 inches) or tall enough (above 12 inches) that they don't block eyes.

Material & Care

If choosing glass, commit to hand-washing to keep the finish sparkling. If you opt for metal, look for sets with protective pads—your table surface will thank you.

Taper Compatibility

Always check your candle diameter against the holder's cup size. If a candle is slightly thin, a small dab of 'candle tack' or a tiny bit of melted wax at the base will keep it standing straight.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these safe for dinner tables with kids?

Look for options with the widest bases, such as the NUPTIO wood or the heavy-bottomed stone holders, which are much harder to nudge over.

Do I need to polish metal holders?

Not for most brushed or matte finishes. A simple microfiber cloth wipe after a dinner is sufficient to remove dust or stray wax.

Can I mix and match different sets?

Absolutely. Mixing the amber glass holders with the matte black iron ones can create a layered, modern aesthetic that feels very intentional.
