BUYER'S GUIDE

What to Consider Before You Choose

Counter Clearance

Before purchasing, measure the height from your countertop to the bottom of your upper cabinets. Many tilt-head mixers need vertical clearance to open fully.

Maintenance & Cleaning

Look for units with dishwasher-safe attachments, but remember that matte ceramic or soft-touch finishes often require a quick hand-wipe to stay looking pristine.

Motor Material

Focus on the wattage, but also look for 'planetary' mixing action. This ensures the attachment hits all areas of the bowl, preventing the frustration of stopping to scrape the sides.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I really need a 6-quart mixer for everyday use?

Unless you are baking for a large family or frequently making double batches of bread, a 3-5 quart model is much more efficient for the average kitchen.

How can I prevent my small mixer from moving while in use?

Look for models with silicone suction cup feet. They provide excellent grip on stone and laminate countertops, keeping the unit steady at higher speeds.

Are stand mixers dishwasher safe?

Generally, the stainless steel bowls are dishwasher safe, but the beaters and hooks should be checked—many aluminum ones are hand-wash only to prevent oxidation.
