BUYER'S GUIDE

What to Consider Before You Choose

Measure Your Cavity

Check the interior dimensions of your oven before purchasing. A 17-inch pan may fit in the oven but can be tricky to maneuver if your oven door clearance is tight.

Material Matters

Stainless steel is best for longevity and high-heat searing, while carbon steel with nonstick coating is superior for easy cleanup and consistent, gentle roasting.

Handle Ergonomics

Always look for riveted handles that provide a wide enough gap for your oven mitts. When moving a 15-pound bird, a secure grip is a safety necessity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I store my roasting pan with the rack inside?

To save space, store the rack inside the pan, but place a soft cloth or paper towel between them to prevent the rack from scratching the pan's nonstick interior.

Is a stainless steel pan better than nonstick for roasting?

Stainless steel is superior for building pan sauces and high-heat searing, whereas nonstick is the undisputed king of easy cleanup and low-maintenance roasting.

How can I tell if a pan will fit in my apartment oven?

Measure the oven rack width and depth, then account for an extra inch of clearance on all sides to ensure heat can circulate properly around the pan.
