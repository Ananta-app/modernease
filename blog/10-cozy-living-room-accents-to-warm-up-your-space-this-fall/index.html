BUYER'S GUIDE

What to Consider Before You Choose

Check the Weave Density

For high-traffic areas, look for 'tight' or 'cable' knits to minimize snagging. Open, 'holey' designs are beautiful but fragile, best reserved for accent decor.

Material Matters

Organic cotton provides the best breathability for year-round use, while synthetic chenille offers superior softness and durability against pilling.

Ease of Maintenance

Always check for 'machine wash/gentle' labels. Avoid dry-clean-only items for daily drivers, as the convenience factor is critical for a clutter-free, functional home.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I prevent my chunky knit blanket from snagging?

Store your blanket away from sharp furniture edges, and remove rings or loose jewelry before lounging. Use a fabric shaver gently if small loops do pull.

Can I wash chenille blankets in the machine?

Most chenille blankets are machine washable on a cold, gentle cycle. Always use a laundry bag to prevent the knit from catching on the drum.

What is the best way to fold a throw for a minimalist look?

Try a 'waterfall' drape over the arm of the sofa or a simple folded rectangle placed at a 45-degree angle on an accent chair to keep it looking intentional.
